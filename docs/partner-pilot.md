# Partner conversation

NearSignal uses one source-linked verification method that can be applied in different markets. Official national facility records provide a foundation; services, age limits, language support, hours and financial assistance require local review. A record in the index is not a promise that a facility can receive a particular patient today.

For CommonSpirit: propose one market with a willing operational champion and community partner. Agree on the population, local evidence review, patient workflow and evaluation before expansion. Inclusion of public facility information does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

Measure successful connections to appropriate care, failed attempts, patient understanding, safety and staff burden. Reduced avoidable emergency-department use is a possible later research outcome, not a demonstrated benefit or the primary promise of an early pilot.

The public brief is carried by the patient. Receiving-team integration is a future partnership pathway, subject to agreed privacy, consent and workflow arrangements.
