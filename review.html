CMS emergency-hospital review queue

Promotion requirements

  1. Confirm the official facility identity and location.
  2. Find an authoritative provider or government source describing the emergency service and whether it serves adults, children, or both.
  3. Verify hours, age limits where relevant, phone, and location-level capabilities.
  4. Add claim-level evidence and a review deadline.
  5. Pass automated safety validation before publication.
